Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

This policy covers Saksi Upload, the internal tool the Saksi Sejarah team uses to publish to its own YouTube channel (@SaksiSejarah-Nusantara). Saksi Upload uses the YouTube API Services.

Who uses it

Only the Saksi Sejarah team, signed in with the Google account that manages the Saksi Sejarah channel. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload our own videos and thumbnails to our channel and set their title, description, tags and publish time.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to add subtitle tracks (captions) to those videos.

It does not read, download or analyse anyone else's videos, comments, subscribers or personal data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the team's own computer, in a file outside any shared folder. It is used only to upload and schedule our own content.
  • We do not collect, sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising or to train any model.
  • Upload responses (such as a video ID) are kept in our own project records to track what has been published.

Third-party terms

By using Saksi Upload we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google account settings, "Third-party apps and services"). The stored token is then deleted from the team's computer.
